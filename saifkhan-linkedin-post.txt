A year ago, that number was 14%.

The businesses that moved early aren't just "staying current." They're seeing measurable outcomes — companies using AI-driven personalization are generating 40% more revenue than their competitors.

After 48 projects across 7 years, I see the same pattern repeat:

→ Founders treat AI as a "Phase 2" feature
→ Phase 2 never comes — or comes too late
→ A competitor ships it first

Here's what the data actually says in 2025:

✅ 51% of developers use AI tools every single day (Stack Overflow, 2025)
✅ AI-assisted teams complete tasks 55.8% faster
✅ App-level AI adoption doubled in less than 12 months

This isn't a trend. It's a shift in what "standard" looks like.

The gap between AI-enabled products and everything else is widening every quarter. Building it in now is significantly cheaper than retrofitting it later.

If you're planning a web app and haven't mapped where AI fits — that's the first conversation worth having.
